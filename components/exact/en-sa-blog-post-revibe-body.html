Unveiling success: Revibe's journey with Tamara towards growth and brand recognition

Creating trust in second-hand electronics

Revibe was founded in July 2022 by Abdessamad Benzakour and Hamza Iraqui, who identified the need to create trust in the regional second-hand electronics market. Their vision was to create a reliable platform where customers could confidently purchase refurbished and pre-loved electronic devices.

Commitment to sustainability and accessibility

At its core, Revibe is dedicated to combating electronic waste by offering refurbished and pre-loved electronic devices. By extending the lifespan of these gadgets, they mitigate environmental impact and provide customers with budget-friendly options without compromising on quality. Their overarching goal is to effect positive change in the world while ensuring tech accessibility for all.

THE CHALLENGE

Overcoming cart abandonment

Revibe encountered the common obstacle of cart abandonment, particularly concerning high-value electronics, stemming from consumer hesitations.  They needed an advertising solution that not only drove traffic but also facilitated completed purchases and increased brand recognition.
CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE

Scaling growth and reducing costs

Achieve 1,000+ orders through Tamara Ads
Reduce the cost per order
Leverage Tamara’s platform to drive growth, boost brand visibility and optimize advertising efficiency
THE SOLUTION

Leveraging Tamara Ads

Tamara Ads emerged as the ideal remedy for Revibe’s challenges. By utilizing Farah Discounts, which focuses on discount-led promotions and targeted audience engagement, Revibe effectively reached potential customers while also elevating their brand profile. Premium placements on Tamara’s platform, including Sponsored Store spots and Sponsored Product listings, further bolstered their visibility.
