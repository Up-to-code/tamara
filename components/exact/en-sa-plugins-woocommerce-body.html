No, the plugin is completely free and will remain so. To activate WooCommerce split payments and installment options, simply sign up for a Tamara account.

How do I add Tamara to my WooCommerce payment options?

Adding Tamara to your WooCommerce payment methods is simple:

  1. Sign up: Create your Tamara account by registering as a Tamara Partner.
  2. Install the plugin: Add our WooCommerce payment gateway to your store in just a few clicks.
  3. Follow the setup guide: Use our documentation for smooth integration.
