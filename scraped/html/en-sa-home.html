Questions you might ask

What is Tamara and how does it work?

Tamara is a payment solution that makes shopping simpler, smarter, and hassle-free. With Tamara, shop leading brands and split your payments with ease!

Where can I use Tamara?

Tamara is available at most stores across Saudi Arabia and the UAE. You can use it online and in-store to enjoy a smooth, and flexible payment experience.

Can I use Tamara both online and in-store?

Yes, Tamara is available both online and in-store, ensuring you have access to flexible payment solutions everywhere you go.

How do I sign up for Tamara?

Signing up for Tamara is simple! Navigate to the Tamara website or app, share your phone number, verify your details, and begin shopping!

What are Exclusive Deals?

With Exclusive Deals, enjoy special offers from brands you know and love, all in one place. Exclusive Deals can be applied automatically at checkout or redeemed using promo codes on the store's checkout page.

What products and services are available with Tamara?

With Tamara, you can split your payments for a variety of products and services. Tamara's payment solutions are offered by a wide range of stores, from beauty to electronics.

How do I complete my payments?

You can complete your payments for your Tamara orders directly via the Tamara app or website. Don’t worry – you’ll receive regular payment reminders from us before your payment is due to help you stay on track!

What happens if I miss one of my payments?

To help you make your payments on time, we make sure to consistently send you payment reminders via push notification and SMS. If you do miss a payment, no late fees will be charged whatsoever.

However, please note that repeated delays in payment may result in the suspension of your Tamara account and an impact on your credit score.

Is my personal information secure with Tamara?

Yes, Tamara prioritizes the security and privacy of all your personal data. All transactions and data are protected using advanced encryption technologies to ensure your details remain safe and secure.
