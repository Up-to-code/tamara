Questions you might ask

How can my business qualify for BNPL?

Whether you're a freelancer, a homegrown brand, or a large enterprise, Tamara makes it simple to get started with buy now pay later for business. We proudly support businesses of all sizes, helping grow the local ecosystem across the GCC.‍

Who can apply?

Tamara works with freelancers and independent sellers, in-store businesses, online stores and social sellers (Instagram, Google Maps, etc.).‍

Required documentation:

  • ‍Valid ID (passport or national ID)
  • Proof of Business Registration:‍
    • Commercial Registration (CR) with a Business IBAN
    • Or Freelance Certificate with a Personal IBAN‍
  • Online Presence:‍
    • A working website, or
    • A public Instagram account, or
    • A Google Maps listing that showcases your products or services

Note: In some cases, Tamara may request additional documents to complete the application.

How does Tamara ensure Sharia-compliant BNPL transactions?

Tamara follows Islamic finance principles through a strict no-interest model and ethical transaction structure. All processes are  reviewed and approved by an independent Sharia Committee.

What makes it Sharia-compliant

  • No interest or hidden fees: Customers pay the same amount when they choose to split their payments.
  • Reviewed by experts: Tamara’s contracts, operations, and financial products are reviewed and approved by a Sharia Committee.
  • Ongoing oversight: The committee continuously monitors operations and advises on product development to ensure ongoing compliance. 

Tamara operates under license from the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) and maintains full Sharia approval across all its core BNPL services.
