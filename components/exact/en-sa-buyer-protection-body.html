Who is eligible?

To qualify for Tamara’s Buyer Protection, your order must meet these criteria
Buyer Protection is available exclusively for Tamara's Pay in Full option.
Your order was placed with a Tamara partner within the last 120 days.
Your order was placed online. In-store orders with scheduled deliveries are only covered in cases of returns. However, in-store purchases that are collected immediately are not covered.
Your order is a Custom-made item, it will only be covered if it wasn’t delivered.
Your account has not been flagged by Tamara for potential fraudulent activity or abuse.
You’ve provided all required evidence, like receipts or communication with the store.

What’s not covered?

Certain types of purchases aren’t eligible for Buyer Protection, including
Payments for services like travel, events, or insurance.
Digital products like subscriptions or NFTs.
Goods like gift cards or digital wallet top-ups.

Questions you might ask

How to track a claim?

You can easily check the status of your claim through the Tamara app. Here’s how:

  1. Log in to your Tamara account.
  2. Go to My Purchases (or Transactions for Smart members).
  3. Select the order for which you raised a claim.
  4. Click on View Details next to the order.
  5. You’ll find the claim status under "Claims".
When will I receive my refund?

If your order is eligible for a refund, it will be processed as soon as the store updates your order status on your Tamara account.

  • If the refund amount is less than your remaining payments:

Your payment plan will be adjusted, and you can view the updated schedule in the Tamara app.

  • If the refund amount is more than your remaining payments:

Any outstanding payments will be canceled, and the remaining balance will be refunded to your Tamara Wallet.

Could my claim be rejected?

When you submit a claim, we carefully review all the provided information and escalate it to the store for resolution.

In some cases, the store may decide that the claim cannot be accepted.

However, if your order qualifies for Buyer Protection, Tamara will carry out its own investigation. If necessary, we’ll issue a refund to ensure you're taken care of.

Can I cancel my claim?

In case you want to cancel a claim you raised for an order, you can easily do that through the Tamara app, by following the below steps:

  1. Sign in to your Tamara account.
  2. Go to “My Purchases”.
  3. Go to “Orders”, and choose the order you want to cancel it's claim
  4. Scroll down and you will see the raised claim.
  5. Click on the claim and choose "Cancel Dispute"
  6. Confirm cancellation
