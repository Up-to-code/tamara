Questions you might ask

What is Tamara Smart?

Tamara Smart is our premium membership program, and it’s here to make payments easier than ever!

Our UAE beloved customers, for a monthly fee of AED 19, you can now unlock a world of benefits, and cancel at any time (T&Cs apply).

Tamara Smart benefits include:

  • Exclusive cashback: Get 1% cashback on every order, up to AED 250 every month.
  • Buyer protection: Order not as expected? Don't worry — we'll take care of any order issues you may experience.
  • Exclusive rewards: Enjoy cashback and offers from your favorite stores.
  • Processing fees: Enjoy your purchases with a processing fee waiver of up to 1%.
How do I sign up for Tamara Smart?

To sign up for Tamara Smart, simply place an order with any Tamara stores and follow the steps below:

  1. Select your preferred Tamara payment plan at checkout.
  2. Tap on the Tamara Smart option to activate your membership.
  3. Complete checking out with Tamara and your Tamara Smart membership will be automatically activated.

How do I cancel Tamara Smart?

Going so soon? We’re sorry to hear that!

You can cancel Tamara Smart membership at any time.

If you’re sure you’d like to cancel your Tamara Smart membership, here’s how to do it:

  1. Go to the Tamara app
  2. Tap “More”
  3. Tap “Membership”
  4. Navigate to Tamara Smart and click “Cancel membership”

Please note that once you’ve canceled your Tamara Smart membership, you’ll still have access to your Tamara Smart benefits until the next billing cycle. Still have questions?

Please don’t hesitate to reach out to us via the Tamara Help Center.
