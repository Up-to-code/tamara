Operating Principles

These principles guide how we think, work, and lead at Tamara.
Customer Obsessed
We exist to serve real people with real needs.
We solve for long-term customer value, not short-term wins.
We build for trust, not dependency, and we treat it as our most valuable.
Seek Truth, Not Comfort
Good ideas can come from anyone.
We debate to learn, not to win, and commit fully once decisions are made.
It’s All About Talent
Exceptional outcomes come from exceptional teams.
We hire with rigor, invest in growth, and lead with respect.
Build Depth, Ignore Noise
Focus is a strategy, not a limitation. We don't chase every opportunity, confuse activity with progress or dilute focus with side projects.
Evolve with Purpose
Change is constant. Direction is intentional.
We question the status quo to serve our mission better. We evolve deliberately and resist change for its own sake.
One Team, One Mission
We operate as one company, not silos. Peers are partners, not obstacles. Trust is built through alignment, shared goals, and honest collaboration.
Communicate Clearly & Set Expectations
Clear expectations build trust and drive results.
We reduce complexity wherever possible. If something is confusing, it is not done.
Get Things Done
Progress matters more than perfection, and learning sharpens execution. Ownership means long-term thinking, accountability, and building scalable systems.
Set the Bar, Enable the Climb
Clear North Star with flexible navigation. We think big, but we test smart. Leaders set high standards and create the conditions for teams to succeed.
Decide with Conviction, Own the Outcome
Data informs judgment but does not delay it. We make decisions with conviction and move forward together. Ownership includes adapting when evidence changes.
