Tamara Whistleblower Policy

At Tamara, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and ethical conduct in everything we do. If you become aware of any conduct that may violate laws, regulations, company policies, or ethical standards, including but not limited to fraud, corruption, harassment, safety breaches, or data misuse, we encourage you to report it immediately.

While we understand whistleblowing feels intimidating, speaking up about misconduct, malpractice or unethical behavior helps us protect our employees, customers, partners, and the integrity of our business.

How to Report

You can submit your concerns confidentially via email: whistleblowingksa@tamara.co

When submitting your report, please include as much factual information as possible, such as:

  • A clear description of the activity or incident
  • Names of individuals involved (if known)
  • Dates, times, and locations of the incident
  • Any supporting evidence or documents you possess

Confidentiality and Protection

We understand that voicing concerns, especially anonymously, requires trust. We ensure that your report will be:

  • Safe: Our system is designed to keep your identity private, ensuring you can speak without fear of exposure.
  • Confidential: All submissions are treated with the highest level of sensitivity and discretion.
  • Protected: We strictly prohibit and do not tolerate any form of retaliation or unfair treatment against individuals who report concerns in good faith.
  • Heard: Every complaint is taken seriously and will be addressed promptly and fairly in accordance with local laws and our Whistleblower Policy.

Important

  • This channel is intended only for whistleblowing reports, such as serious ethical breaches or violations of company policies.
  • If your concern relates to your Tamara account, payments, transactions, customer support, or suspected fraud, please contact the  Help Center.

Your voice protects the people around you — and the values we all believe in.

Thanks for helping us keep Tamara safe, fair, and transparent.
