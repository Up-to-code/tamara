Tamara allows customers to split high-value purchases into easy installments while you receive the full payment upfront. Tamara handles all billing and collection, letting you focus on growing your brand.

Which types of jewellery businesses can use Tamara?

We support all types of jewellery boutiques, luxury watch stores, fashion accessory brands, and online retailers selling fine or designer pieces.

Is Tamara available online and in-store?

Yes. Tamara integrates with major ecommerce platforms and also supports in-store checkout via POS and payment links.

When does the business receive the payment?

You receive the full sale amount instantly, while Tamara collects payments directly from your customers.

Is there any risk or liability for my business?

No. Tamara covers all credit and collection risk. You sell securely, and we handle the rest.
