Questions you might ask

Do I need to pay an installation fee for Tamara’s Shopify payment app?

No. Our Shopify payment gateway payment app is completely free and will stay that way. To access Tamara’s flexible payment options, just sign up for a Tamara account.

How do I add Tamara to my Shopify store?

Adding Tamara to your Shopify payment options is simple:

  1. Sign up: Create your Tamara account by registering as a Tamara Partner.
  2. Install the payment app: Add our payment gateway to your Shopify store in just a few clicks.
  3. Review documentation: Follow our step-by-step guides for seamless integration.
Do I need a developer to add Tamara to my Shopify website?

No. Adding Tamara is a straightforward payment integration that doesn’t require any coding. Check out our step-by-step guide here.

How long does it take to hear back about my application?

Once you submit your application, you’ll join our waiting list. Our Tamara team will reach out within a few business days with an update on your application status.

What happens if a customer doesn’t pay?

No worries! Tamara handles the payment settlements with merchants every week, regardless of customer payments. We’ve got it covered so you don’t have to!

How long does it take to receive my settlement?

We offer two types of settlements:

  • Flexible settlement: Issued on Saturdays if your settlement value (order minus Tamara fee) is SAR/AED 2,500 or more. You’ll receive it in your bank account by the following Tuesday with no bank charges.
  • Weekly settlement: Sent every Tuesday. For settlements under SAR/AED 2,500, a SAR/AED 25 bank transfer fee is deducted.

Rest assured, we settle your accounts in full regardless of whether the customer has paid.
