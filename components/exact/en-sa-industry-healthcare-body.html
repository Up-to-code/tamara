Questions you might ask

How can a clinic offer installment payment plans to patients?

Activate Tamara and offer installment payments at checkout. Patients choose to split the bill, while your clinic receives the full payment upfront.

What is buy now pay later for healthcare providers? How does it work?

Buy now pay later allows patients to pay over time. Your clinic or hospital receives the full amount upfront, and Tamara handles the collection and risk.

Can Tamara integrate with our ERP or health information system?

Yes. Tamara provides APIs and ready integrations so your finance and tech teams can reconcile payments automatically.

Is Tamara available for pharmacies and wellness centers?

Yes. Tamara supports split payments for pharmacies, wellness centers, dental clinics, cosmetic surgery centers, and more.

How does flexible payment help increase treatment acceptance rates?

When patients can pay in smaller, manageable parts, they’re more likely to commit to full treatment plans, reducing cancellations and delays.
