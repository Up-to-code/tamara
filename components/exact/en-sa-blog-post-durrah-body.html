Durrah Flooring's journey from B2B giant to consumer favorite

Uncover the story behind Durrah Flooring’s exponential growth in just one year.

From wholesale roots to retail disruption

For years, Durrah Flooring, a subsidiary of Al-Sourayi International, has been a leading force in the interior design industry, delivering premium solutions to various businesses.
Recently, Durrah Flooring recognized significant opportunities within the B2C market, making its products more accessible to a broader range of customers.
THE OBJECTIVE

Fast-tracking B2C market penetration

Durrah Flooring entered the B2C space with ambitious objectives: rapidly increase consumer conversions and expand market share within a tight timeframe.
Success demanded a smarter, more strategic route to market; one designed to generate visibility, build trust, and convert demand at scale.
THE CHALLENGE

Reaching out to a new audience

The obstacles were clear: the brand’s familiarity in the retail space was limited, consumer trust needed to be built from scratch, and demand for higher-ticket items faced natural resistance. For a brand transitioning from B2B dominance to consumer retail, these barriers threatened to slow growth.
THE SOLUTION

Meeting consumers’ concerns with extra flexibility

To overcome buyer hesitation, Durrah Flooring offered more than just premium quality. It introduced flexibility. This is where the partnership with Tamara proved pivotal.

In-store marketing educated customers on flexible payments (above credit limit available in KSA only).

Higher credit limits encouraged full room makeovers over smaller buys.

Extended payment plans of up to 12 months made premium purchases more accessible.

THE OUTCOME

Measurable sales results within just one year!
