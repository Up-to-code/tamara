Questions you might ask

What is the relationship between Tamara and Geidea?

Tamara works with Geidea as a payment partner, allowing businesses to offer flexible payment options across online and in store channels.

Is Geidea setup and Tamara activation easy?

Yes. If you already use Geidea, Tamara can be activated quickly with minimal steps. Our team will guide you if needed.

Does Geidea support online and in store payments?

Yes. Geidea supports online checkout, payment links, and in store POS. Tamara works across all these channels.

Do I need a technical team to get started?

No. Tamara is easy to activate and does not require complex technical work or development resources.
