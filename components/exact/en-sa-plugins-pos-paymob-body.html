Questions you might ask

How does Tamara integrate with Paymob?

Tamara is a value-added partner of Paymob, with a fully embedded integration within the Paymob dashboard and payment solutions.

Businesses can activate Tamara easily for both online and in-store payments, with no additional technical integration required.

Is Paymob setup and Tamara activation easy?

Yes. If you already have a Paymob account, Tamara can be activated in just a few steps directly from the dashboard.

If you don’t have an account yet, our team is happy to support you through the sign-up process.

Does Paymob support online and in-store payments?

Yes. Paymob supports online checkout, in-store POS payments, and payment links.

With Tamara, you can offer flexible payment options across all these channels through one integrated solution.

Do I need technical support or a development team?

No. The sign-up and activation process is simple and supported by a dedicated Paymob account manager.

Since Tamara is already built into Paymob’s solutions, all you need to do is activate it once your account is set up.
